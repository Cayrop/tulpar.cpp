Trip-to-trip boundary
---------------------
Only v12 is loop-carried. v0, v1 (sumi), all v_dot4 inputs/accums, all grid values,
all sign patterns are dead by the next trip start (0x1485BC).

Verdict
-------
1. vmcnt(0) at 0x148D40 is REQUIRED, not conservative. It is the minimum wait for L14
   (v58), the last-issued load, which is consumed at 0x148D44 (v_xor v52, v58, v17)
   only ~4 cycles later. Without it, the 8th grid gather produces stale data.
   The wait is also sufficient for L13 (v57) which is used at 0x148EE4.
   The wait is also sufficient for L6 (v0) used at 0x1490F4-1490F8.
2. vmcnt(9) at 0x148720 is REQUIRED, gating L1..L5. v[0:3] and v[4:7] (L2, L3) feed
   the 8 v_dot4s; v50 (L5) feeds the sign bit pattern; v16 (L1) feeds the last v_dot4.
3. vmcnt(2) at 0x148678 is REQUIRED, gating L1..L4 for v[23:24] sign extraction.
4. The 6 ladder waitcnts W(7)..W(2) at 0x148D04-0x148D2C are well-placed: each one is
   paired with the very next v_xor that consumes its corresponding grid load
   (L7..L12 -> v51..v56). They are not conservative - they are exactly the minimum
   wait for each producer.
5. The 8 grid gathers (L7..L14) are independent of each other: each uses a different
   q3 byte as index, and the table base s[8:9] is invariant. They are issued together
   in s_clause 0x7, and only the xor+sub+perm chain that *uses* each load is what
   makes the 8 gather results semantically separate.
6. No s_barrier, no ds_*, no extra lgkmcnt barriers. The only barrier between trips
   is the implicit register dependence on v12. There is no excessive compiler
   fence in the K-loop.
